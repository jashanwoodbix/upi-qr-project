The journey

Five steps, one payment

Here's what happens when a customer pays a shop using a UPI QR code.

01

Scan the QR

The customer opens a UPI app and scans the merchant's QR code.

02

Check details

The app shows the receiver's details. The customer enters the amount and checks it carefully.

03

Authorize

The customer confirms the payment with their UPI PIN inside the payment app.

04

Process request

The UPI system and participating banks process the payment request.

05

Get confirmation

If the transaction succeeds, the app displays a payment confirmation.

Remember

Digital payment safety

🔐 Keep your UPI PIN private

Never share your PIN with anyone. Enter it only in your trusted UPI app when authorizing a payment.

👀 Check before paying

Verify the receiver's name and the amount before confirming a transaction.

✅ Check the final status

Always check the transaction status in your payment app instead of relying only on a sound or screenshot.
