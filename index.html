Mission 02 · The big reveal

So, how does UPI actually work?

Think of UPI as a secure digital messenger that helps banks move money between accounts.

STEP 01
📷

Scan the QR

The QR code shares payment details, such as the receiver's UPI ID and sometimes an amount.

STEP 02
📱

Check & enter

Your UPI app shows who you're paying. You check the name and enter the amount if needed.

STEP 03
🔐

Approve securely

You approve the payment using your UPI PIN inside the app. Never share this PIN with anyone.

STEP 04
🏦

Banks communicate

The UPI system routes the request between participating banks and checks whether it can be processed.

STEP 05
✅

Money & message

If successful, the sender's account is debited and the receiver's account is credited; both may get confirmation.

🛣️ Follow the payment journey

👤 You→📲 UPI app→🔁 UPI network→🏦 Banks→🛍️ Receiver

The QR code doesn't itself carry money. It helps the app identify the payee. The payment is processed electronically through the UPI and banking systems after you authorize it.

Mission 03 · Make it memorable

Imagine a digital relay race

Each part has a different job.

🏷️

QR code = Address card

It helps your app find the person or shop you want to pay—like an address on a parcel.

🛡️

UPI PIN = Your approval

It authorizes a payment. Keep it secret; a shopkeeper or support agent should never ask you to reveal it.

🏦

Banks = Money keepers

The linked bank accounts are where money is debited and credited when a real payment succeeds.
